```
◆ 정확도 측정 · AI 진단
별도 장비 없이 iPhone 마이크만으로 기계식 시계의 일오차(rate)·비트 에러·진폭을 측정합니다. 신뢰도 등급(A~F)과 AI 해석으로 결과를 쉽게 이해하고, 폰 시계 자동 보정으로 정밀도를 높였습니다. 깨끗한 신호에서 같은 자세 단일 측정은 약 ±2초/일, 여러 번 평균 시 ±1초/일까지 좁힐 수 있습니다. 자세별 편차 분석으로 워치메이커처럼 정비 시점을 가늠하세요.

◆ 컬렉션 관리
시계마다 스펙·사진·시리얼·구매가와 재무(총 소유비용)를 정리하고, 스트랩·정비 기록을 남기세요. 오버홀 시기와 보증 만료(최대 10년·만료 전·당일 알림)를 자동으로 알려드립니다.

◆ 분석
주간·월간·연간 트렌드, 월별 착용 heatmap, 연말 Wrapped(올해의 시계 이야기), 글로벌 브랜드 리그로 내 시계 생활을 한눈에 봅니다.
```

## Description (≤4000)
```
TickLab — the watch-life platform to record, manage, analyze and share every moment with your watches.

It started with accuracy measurement, but TickLab now holds a collector's whole day. Five pillars in one app: measure, collection, life log, insights, and community.

◆ Accuracy measurement · AI diagnosis
Measure rate, beat error and amplitude of your mechanical watch using only your iPhone's microphone — no extra hardware. A reliability grade (A–F) and AI interpretation make results easy to read, and automatic phone-clock calibration improves precision. On a clean signal, a single same-position measurement lands around ±2 s/day, narrowing to ±1 s/day when you average several. Positional-variance analysis helps you judge service timing like a watchmaker.

◆ Collection management
Organize specs, photos, serial number, purchase price and finances (total cost of ownership) for each watch, and keep strap and service records. Get automatic reminders for overhaul timing and warranty expiration (up to 10 years, with before- and on-expiry alerts).

◆ Life log
Capture moments with wear logs, a journal, memory highlights, occasion tags and moods. Dream up your next piece with a wishlist.

◆ Insights
See your watch life at a glance with weekly/monthly/yearly trends, a wear heatmap, a year-end Wrapped, and a global brand league.

◆ Anonymous community (17+)
Like, comment and follow on a nickname-free photo feed and collect badges. Kept safe with reporting, blocking and content filtering.

◆ Home Screen widget · Live Activity
View your latest measurement in a widget and log today's wear right from it. During a measurement it shows live on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.

— Privacy —
Your measurement data, serial numbers and purchase prices stay on your device and are never transmitted. Community photos are uploaded only after you tap Post, with location (EXIF) data stripped.

— TickLab Pro —
Start free, and unlock everything with a monthly or yearly subscription.
• TickLab Pro Monthly — auto-renews every month
• TickLab Pro Yearly — auto-renews every year
Prices follow the amounts shown on the App Store for your region. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at purchase confirmation. Subscriptions auto-renew unless turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
```
