### Description
TickLab is the watch life platform to record, manage, analyze and share every moment with your watches.

It started with accuracy measurement, but now it brings measurement, collection, life log, insights and community together in one app.

Accuracy measurement and AI diagnosis
Measure the rate, beat error and amplitude of your mechanical watch using only your iPhone microphone, with no extra hardware. A reliability grade from A to F and AI interpretation make results easy to read, and automatic phone clock calibration improves precision. On a clean signal, a single measurement in the same position lands within about 2 seconds a day, narrowing to about 1 second a day when you average several. Positional variance analysis helps you judge service timing.

Collection management
Organize specs, photos, serial number, purchase price and finances for each watch, and keep strap and service records. Get automatic reminders for overhaul timing and warranty expiration. Warranty length can be set up to 10 years, with reminders before and on the expiry day.

Life log
Capture your moments with wear logs, a journal, memory highlights, occasion tags and moods. Dream up your next watch with a wishlist.

Insights
See your watch life at a glance with weekly, monthly and yearly trends, a monthly wear heatmap, a year end review and a global brand league.

Anonymous community, ages 17 and up
Like, comment and follow on a nickname free photo feed and collect badges. Kept safe with reporting, blocking and content filtering.

Home Screen widget and Live Activity
View your latest measurement in a widget and log today's wear right from it. During a measurement it shows live on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.
